Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Oshabe is open source under the MIT license. There is no account and no subscription.

Does my audio get uploaded?

No. Speech recognition runs on your computer. Meeting audio is never saved, only the text.

Do I need to set up AI keys?

No. A built-in offline model handles cleanup and summaries. You can switch to Ollama or your own provider if you want.

Will it warn me that my computer is not trusted?

Oshabe is not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen or macOS Gatekeeper may show a warning on first run. Choose "More info, Run anyway" on Windows, or right-click, Open on Mac.

Who makes it?

Oshabe is built by Hikari Webworks in Hyderabad, as a fork of the open-source Handy project.
