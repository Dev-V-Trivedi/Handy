Meeting notes

Works with any call, because it listens to the computer, not the app.

Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Slack huddles and browser calls all play through your speakers or headphones. Oshabe records that audio next to your microphone.

Start

One key or one click

Use your shortcut, the tray menu or the Start button. Oshabe can also ask when it notices a call.

During

Live transcript, both sides

Your voice and the other people are captured separately and labelled You and Others. Pause and resume any time.

After

Summary you can send

Decisions and action items, ready to copy or export as Word or PDF. Old notes delete themselves after the days you choose.

Dictation

Talk, and clean text lands where your cursor is.

Hold the shortcut to talk and release to stop. Tap it once to go hands-free, tap again to finish.

Fast or accurate, your pick

Choose a preset and Oshabe downloads the right speech model once. It then works with no internet.

Cleanup without keys

A built-in offline model removes filler words and fixes punctuation. No account, no API key.

Your words, spelled right

Add names, products and jargon so they come out correctly every time.

Privacy

Your audio stays on your computer.

Speech recognition runs locally. For meetings, Oshabe keeps text only and never saves the recording.

  • RecordingNever stored. Only the transcript text is saved.
  • Where notes liveA folder on your computer. Export to Word or PDF when you want a copy.
  • Network useDownloading models and checking for updates. Nothing else unless you pick a cloud provider.
  • LicenceMIT. Read the code on GitHub.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no subscription, no limits. It is open source under the MIT licence.

Do I need an API key or Ollama?

No. A built-in offline model handles cleanup and summaries. You can switch to Ollama or your own provider in Settings.

Will the other people on the call know?

Oshabe does not join the call, so no one sees a bot. Laws about recording differ by country, so tell people when you are taking notes.

Why is English sometimes typed in another language?

Automatic language detection can guess wrong on accented English. Set Language to English in Settings and it stays put.

Who makes Oshabe?

Hikari Webworks in Hyderabad. It is a fork of the open source Handy project.
